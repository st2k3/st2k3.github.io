FAQ

Common questions

Are your estimates really free?

Yes. We provide free, no-obligation consultations and written estimates for all projects.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. All of our team members are licensed, insured and background-checked for your peace of mind.

How long does a typical remodel take?

A bathroom usually takes 2–4 weeks and a kitchen 4–8 weeks, depending on scope and materials. You'll get a clear timeline before work begins.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. Our workmanship is backed by a warranty, and we pass along all manufacturer warranties on materials.

Can I stay in my home during the project?

In most cases, yes. We set up dust barriers, protect your floors and keep the work area clean and safe each day.
